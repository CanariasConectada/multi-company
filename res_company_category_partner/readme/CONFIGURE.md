1. Go to *Settings > Users & Companies > Users*.
2. On the user form, enable **Company Category Manager** (under *Other
   Extra Rights / Company Categories*).
3. The user also needs the usual right to edit contacts (*Contact
   Creation* / contact editing access) to save the partner form.

Administrators (*Settings* access) are members of the group implicitly and
are the only ones allowed to create root categories, from
*Settings > Technical* or the `res_company_category` menus.
